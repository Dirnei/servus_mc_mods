# Servus: Notes changelog

## 0.1.0

### Added
- A notebook opened with N, no item needed: projects, their tasks and a page per task, in a leather bound book.
- Tasks with subtasks, notes and an assignee; drag tasks to reorder them or make them subtasks; ticks record who and when.
- Private, Team (FTB Teams parties, optional) and Server projects, plus guests who may edit or only read; ops move projects with `/notes transfer`.
- Item goals with a shared count, a bar that also shows what you carry, a button to add what you carry, and an item picker with JEI drag and drop.
- A named place per task with a beacon beam to walk to it.
- Claimed item tasks tick themselves once you carry everything they still need.
- Pin a project to the HUD (J hides it) and get toasts when someone else ticks a task.
