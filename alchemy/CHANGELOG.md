# Servus: Alchemy changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- The Venter Equinus: a warm straw bed whose Cucurbit putrefies rot into Humus. Form it with the Journal and fill it by clicking any of its blocks.
- Grass Seed from the Athanor turns Dirt into Grass.
- Dirt from Sand, Clay and Humus or Bone Meal in the Mixer.

### Changed
- The journal now sends surplus into the Incinerator, the new name of the Sink.

### Fixed
- The Calciner's fuel upkeep asks for the fuel already in its slot and tops up in batches, like the burner machines.

## 0.4.0-alpha

### Added
- Nether and End matter burns in the Calciner: Netherrack, Soul Sand, Nether Wart and its block, Crimson and Warped Stems, Blaze Rods, Magma Cream, Glowstone Dust, Nether Quartz, End Stone, Purpur, Chorus Fruit, Ender Pearls and Shulker Shells.
- The Purifier washes Sugar, Gunpowder and Redstone into Kandis, Brimstone and Cinnabar, Salt from Dried Kelp, Rotten Flesh, Soul Sand and Magma Cream, and colour out of Concrete Powder, Wool, Carpet and Terracotta.
- The Salamander Forge, Receptaculum, Prelum and Coagulator take a name in their GUI's title row, and with Servus: Shuttles they, the Athanor and the Calciner get a number from the warehouse of their data line ("Athanor #1").

### Changed
- The journal's Calciner table fills in as your team burns things, with hints for the rest.
- The Coagulator grows what its seed is and uses one seed per cycle: a product seeds itself and comes back doubled, while Kandis, Brimstone and Cinnabar turn into Salt Crystal, Sulfur and Quicksilver. The recipe popup is gone.
- Drinking the Elixir of Sight no longer gives back a glass bottle.
- Grown citrine is now a stepped tower with small upward-leaning buds in clear yellows, and buds no longer grow into the tanks beside the vat.

### Fixed
- When several calcination recipes take the same item, the most specific one wins.

## 0.3.0-alpha

### Added
- Citrinitas ritual: place three Principle Vats beside the Athanor's tanks and offer an Amethyst Block; yellow citrine grows out of the vats.
- Rubedo ritual: place four Obsidian behind the tanks and offer a Ghast Tear; dark red Rubedo Obelisks rise and the citrine turns orange.

### Changed
- The Athanor has a new compact build: a 3 by 3 brick hearth with a stair in front, tanks three high and one Copper Duct each; existing Athanors must be rebuilt. Albedo is a Salt Crystal ring under the hearth.
- The formed Athanor is a brick tower with a black iron fire door whose window and dome vents glow in the stage colour.
- A broken part of the Athanor's build drops it back exactly as far as needed, and the crystals and obelisks of the lost stages turn back.

## 0.2.0-alpha

Needs Servus: Foundation 0.3.0 or later.

### Changed
- The Calciner pit and firebox are crafted from deepslate and stone instead of bricks.
- The Athanor is crafted from Stone Bricks, the Coagulator from cobblestone instead of iron.
- The Calciner banks its fire while it has no work instead of burning fuel away.
