## Context

Field work has one job kind so far: the harvest run (`FieldJob`, `HarvestRun` in `ShuttleFleet`). A shuttle's one tool is a `ShuttleModuleItem` with a `ModuleSpec(id, fieldOnly, wide)`; jobs ask for modules through `JobNeeds`, and `ShuttleFleet.assignJobs` hands each job to the nearest free shuttle the needs fit. The warehouse looks at its Field Heads every `field_check_interval` ticks (`WarehouseNetwork.tickFields`) and queues a run when `FieldDue` says so. The harvester fetches seeds from storage before it drives out (`fetchSeeds`, `loadSeeds`) and puts its cargo away with the put-away routing (`continueStore`, `insertAll`, `waitStore`).

The head learns plot states from `FieldEvents` (crop growth, bone meal, break, place, tool, trample) plus a pass on load, and keeps them in `FieldRipeness`. Since `m10-crop-picker` the Field tab chooses the crop through the reusable `ItemPicker`, with stock counts synced by `FieldCropStockPayload` and the choice sent as `ChooseFieldCropPayload` and checked in `FieldHeadMenu.choose`.

Both `m10-field-head-tabs` and `m10-crop-picker` are applied but not archived and archive before this change, in that order. This change modifies the "Field Head GUI" requirement of `storage/field-head`; its delta is written against the newest version, the one `m10-crop-picker` writes (crop picker on the Field tab), and only adds the Fertilizer tab, its marker and its scenarios.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A Spreader tool module and a fertilize run that share the fleet, the fetch and the put-away with the harvester.
- Per head fertilize settings and a reserve, decided at the existing field check, with pure logic covered by JUnit.
- A Fertilizer tab built on `ItemPicker`.

**Non-Goals:**
- No warehouse wide reserve (per field, accepted by the user).
- No per field binding of Spreaders.
- No fertilizing of plots that are not age crops (saplings, grass): only plots of the field the head tracks.
- The head keeps no settings on dismantle today (its loot drops the plain block with its wood), so the new settings do not travel either; they are saved on the block entity.

## Decisions

- **Module.** `ServusStorageItems.SPREADER` = `ShuttleModuleItem(new ModuleSpec("spreader", true, true))`: field only and wide, so the existing liftless ways and the "wide" tooltip line apply. Tooltip key `tooltip.servus_storage.module.spreader`. The workshop takes any `ShuttleModuleItem` as a tool already. Recipe in the spirit of the Harvester (iron, a tool, a hopper): `IHI / IDI / " B "` with iron ingots, a hopper, a dispenser and a bone block; unlocked by having a Shuttle Mk1.
- **Look.** `ShuttleModels.spreader`: model `shuttle/tool_spreader` with two iron arms from the body, a hopper box (hopper outside texture) at the front, a funnel below it and a spreader bar of bone block across the front from x -2 to 18 at the bottom. `ShuttleItemModel` registers tool parts per `ShuttleModuleItem` already, so nothing else changes on the client. Item texture `textures/item/spreader.png`, a placeholder drawn with the helper PNG writer.
- **Job.** `FertilizeJob(BlockPos head)` joins the sealed `ShuttleJob`; `JobNeeds.SPREADER = new JobNeeds(Set.of("spreader"), true)`. A Spreader shuttle has modules `{spreader}`, so `JobNeeds.HARVESTER` does not fit it and the other way round. `assignJobs` treats every job whose needs are field jobs as dropped (not served instantly) when nobody fits it. `ShuttleFleet.hasFieldShuttles`, `fieldReachable` and `fieldWorker` take the `JobNeeds` of the kind; `hasFertilizeJob(head)` sits beside `hasFieldJob(head)`. A harvest run and a fertilize run may serve the same field at once.
- **Runs share a base.** `FieldRun` holds what both kinds need: head, `FieldCargo`, the plots per rail (`reach`), the visited rails, the work queue and countdown, the stacks on their way into storage, the store target and the wait timer. `HarvestRun` extends it with the harvest flags; `SpreadRun` adds the fertilizer key, the doses per plot, the amount still to fetch, the sources tried and the source driven to. A shuttle holds `run` (harvest) or `spread` (fertilize); `continueStore`, `insertAll`, `waitStore`, `anyStorable`, `park`, `dropCargo` and the shown cargo work on either, and after the put-away the fleet turns the run of its kind.
- **Fertilize run legs.** New leg `TO_SUPPLY`; the others are shared (`TO_FIELD`, `WORKING`, `TO_STORE`, `WAIT_STORE`). `spreadTurn` decides: head gone or fertilizing off: put away what it carries, then home. Not fetched yet: amount = `FertilizePlan.toFetch(targets, doses, available, reserve, room)`; while the amount is above zero and an untried source holds the fertilizer, drive to the first one it reaches (sources no rail reaches give at once, racks need a way, storage only reached by lift is skipped, as in `fetchSeeds`). Then: carrying fertilizer and a rail with target plots left, work; else put away, then home. The fetch amount is fixed by the plots that need a dose when the run starts, so the reserve holds at that moment.
- **Dosing.** At a rail the queue is the rail's plots that need a dose now (`FieldHeadBlockEntity.fertilizeTargets()`). Per plot, after the work time: up to `doses_per_plot` times, while the cargo holds fertilizer and the plot is still a growing age crop, `BoneMealItem.applyBonemeal(fertilizer.copyWithCount(1), level, plot, null)`; a successful call takes one item from the cargo and plays `levelEvent(1505)` for the particles. `applyBonemeal` fires the NeoForge `BonemealEvent` that `FieldEvents` already listens to, and the plot is also handed to `head.recheck`, so ripeness updates the next tick. Then `head.dosed(plot)` clears the plot from the newly planted set. In Until ripe share mode the run stops dosing as soon as the head's share reaches the threshold (the targets are empty then). Ripe plots are never targets and `applyBonemeal` refuses them anyway (`isValidBonemealTarget` is false at max age). Every item of the tag counts as one bone meal application: the item is only the cost, the growth is vanilla bone meal growth on that block (wheat grows 2 to 5 ages per dose).
- **Newly planted plots.** `FieldHeadBlockEntity` keeps `unfed`, the plots planted since their last dose. `update(plot, state)` (the event path) adds a plot when it turns from not planted to growing and removes it when it becomes ripe or unplanted; `recheckAll` (load and field changes) never adds. The set is saved as a long array, so a reload does not lose the plots still owed a dose.
- **Pure logic** in `storage/field`, no Minecraft imports:
  - `FertilizeMode` (OFF, AFTER_PLANTING, UNTIL_RIPE) with `byId`.
  - `FertilizePlan.toFetch(plots, doses, stock, reserve, room)` = max(0, min(plots x doses, stock - reserve, room)); `FertilizePlan.targets(mode, unfedGrowing, growing, ripe, planted, percent)` how many plots need a dose; `FertilizePlan.intervalPassed(now, last, interval)` (no call yet counts as passed); `FertilizePlan.due(targets, stock, reserve, now, last, interval)`; `FertilizePlan.stepReserve(reserve, delta)` clamped to 0..`MAX_RESERVE` (9999, fits the container data short).
  - `FertilizeStatus` (OFF, NOT_LINKED, NO_SPREADER, NO_WAY, WORKING, NO_STOCK, BELOW_RESERVE, WAITING) with `of(on, linked, spreader, reachable, working, stock, reserve)` and `problem()` true for NO_SPREADER, NO_WAY, NO_STOCK and BELOW_RESERVE. NO_WAY is a problem as well: a Spreader that never reaches the field is as useless as none.
  - `FieldRipeness.growing()`: planted plots that are not ripe.
- **Call interval.** The head keeps the game time of its last call (`lastFertilizeCall`, saved, -1 for none). `WarehouseNetwork.tickFields` checks the harvester and the Spreader separately; for the Spreader it asks `head.fertilizeDue(now, available)` and on a queued run calls `head.fertilizeCalled(now)`. Nothing runs per tick.
- **Settings and choices.** Saved tags `Fertilizer` (item id), `FertilizeMode`, `FertilizePercent` (0 follows `fertilize_share`), `Reserve` (-1 follows `fertilizer_reserve`), `Unfed`, `FertilizeCalled`. The fertilizer travels in the update tag like the crop, so the menu shows it on the client. `ChooseFertilizerPayload(Optional<ResourceLocation>)` goes to `FieldHeadMenu.chooseFertilizer(player, id)`, which checks the open menu and `stillValid` like `choose` and accepts only items in the tag; empty is Off. Buttons by id: mode Off 3, After planting 4, Until ripe share 5, threshold down 6 and up 7, reserve -10 8, -1 9, +1 10, +10 11; `clickMenuButton` ignores other ids.
- **Stock and status sync.** `FieldFertilizerStockPayload(containerId, networked, counts)` mirrors the crop stock payload for the tag's items and is sent on the same 20 tick timer when its signature changes. Container data slots carry the mode, threshold, reserve and status (ordinal plus one, zero while the head serves no field). `FieldWorkerPayload` gets a second name, the Spreader's plate.
- **Tab layout** (panel 176 by 178): the tab between Water and Upgrades, icon bone meal; Page order FIELD, WATER, FERTILIZER, UPGRADES (Upgrades moves down one tab). Picker at 8,17 (160 wide); mode buttons at y 37 (Off 30, After planting 64, Until ripe 62 wide, the chosen one inactive); "Keep in stock n" at y 53 with -10 -1 +1 +10 at x 94 to 168; "Fertilize below n%" at y 65 with - and + at 136 and 152, shown only in Until ripe share; the status from y 77, two rows. The inventory label is not drawn on this tab to make room for the second status row.
- **Numbers** in `storage/field.json`: `fertilize_interval` 600, `doses_per_plot` 2, `fertilizer_reserve` 64, `fertilize_share` 0.5. `fertilizer_reserve` may be 0 (read with a floor of 0, not 1). Test setters for the interval and the doses.
- **Tag.** `ServusStorageItemTagsProvider`: `servus_storage:fertilizer` with `minecraft:bone_meal`; lang `tag.item.servus_storage.fertilizer`.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Two fields with low reserves can still drain the stock together] Accepted with the per field reserve; the reserve is checked when each run is planned.
- [A run that dosed only part of its targets leaves the rest for the next call] The next call waits for `fertilize_interval`; the targets stay counted.
- [`applyBonemeal` with a null player] NeoForge's `BonemealEvent` allows a null player; mods that cancel bone meal for fake users get their say through the event.
- [Modded crops whose growth per bone meal differs] The run uses whatever `performBonemeal` does; only the item cost is fixed at one per dose.
