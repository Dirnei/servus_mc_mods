## Context

See proposal.md for the why. `:delicacies` exists since `m14-delicacies-project` with a creative tab, the Pretzel, language and item model datagen and a GameTest setup. It has no blocks, loot tables, tags or unit tests yet. Shuttles farms crops through `HarvesterWork` and `FieldHeadBlockEntity`. Sources checked: `minecraft-patched-26.1.2.116-sources.jar` and `neoforge-26.1.2.116-sources.jar`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** six vanilla style crops with seeds, drops, a way to find them, tags, compost values, names and textures; JUnit tests for the crop catalogue; GameTests for growth, drops and the seed sources.

**Non-Goals:** food values, recipes, world generation, any change outside `:delicacies` and the texture script.

## Decisions

### One crop catalogue without Minecraft imports
`crop/CropKind` is a plain Java enum of the six crops. Each entry knows its name, whether it plants itself, and derives its ids: block `<name>_crop`, harvest item (`cabbage`, ..., `mustard_seeds`), planting item (`<name>_seeds` for seeded crops, the harvest for self planting ones), seed item (only for seeded crops and Mustard), tag paths `crops/<name>` and `seeds/<name>`, and the four texture names. `crop/CropStages` maps an age 0 to 7 to the texture stage 0 to 3 exactly like vanilla carrots (`0,0,1,1,2,2,2,3`). Registration, datagen and GameTests loop over `CropKind`, so the six crops cannot drift apart. JUnit covers ids, uniqueness and the stage mapping.

### One crop block class
`crop/DelicaciesCropBlock extends CropBlock` with a `Supplier<ItemLike>` for the planting item, returned from `getBaseSeedId()` (verified: `CropBlock#getBaseSeedId` is `protected ItemLike`, used by `getCloneItemStack`). Ages, the shape per age, random tick growth, light and farmland checks (`mayPlaceOn` uses `BlockTags.SUPPORTS_CROPS`), and bone meal (`BonemealableBlock`, `getBonemealAgeIncrease`, `isValidBonemealTarget` false at max age) all come from `CropBlock` unchanged. Its `MapCodec` reads the planting item by registry name plus the block properties (`RecordCodecBuilder` with `propertiesCodec()`), since `simpleCodec` needs a one argument constructor. Block properties copy vanilla `Blocks.CARROTS`: `mapColor(PLANT)`, `noCollision`, `randomTicks`, `instabreak`, `sound(CROP)`, `pushReaction(DESTROY)`.

### Planting items are block items
Every planting item is a `BlockItem` of its crop block with `Item.Properties#useItemDescriptionPrefix()`, like vanilla `Items.createBlockItemWithCustomItemName`, so its name is `item.servus_delicacies.<id>`. Cabbage, Red Cabbage and Parsley harvests are plain items. This matters for Shuttles: `FieldHeadBlockEntity.isCropItem` and `HarvesterWork.plant` accept an item when it is a `BlockItem` whose block is a `CropBlock`, and `HarvesterWork.classify` treats any `CropBlock` at max age as ripe and rolls its loot table with `Block.getDrops`. So the Field Head lists all six planting items in its crop picker and the Harvester harvests and replants them with no Shuttles change and no tag.

### Loot tables like vanilla
`DelicaciesBlockLoot` (a `BlockLootSubProvider`) uses vanilla `createCropDrops(block, harvest, seed, isMaxAge)` for seeded crops: the harvest when ripe, otherwise the seed, plus seeds by `ApplyBonusCount.addBonusBinomialDistributionCount(fortune, 0.5714286, 3)` when ripe. Self planting crops (Onion, Horseradish, Mustard) use the carrot table shape: one item always, plus the same binomial bonus when ripe, with explosion decay. All numbers are in the generated JSON.

### First seeds through global loot modifiers
Verified in NeoForge 26.1: `AddTableLootModifier` (`neoforge:add_table`, conditions plus a `table` key) rolls a sub table into the target, `LootTableIdCondition` (`neoforge:loot_table_id`) selects the target, `GlobalLootModifierProvider` writes `data/<mod>/loot_modifiers/<name>.json`, and `LootModifierManager` loads every file in that folder (no global list file any more). Two modifiers:
- `grass_seeds`: targets `minecraft:blocks/short_grass` and `minecraft:blocks/tall_grass` (one modifier each, the condition takes one id) and adds `servus_delicacies:gameplay/grass_seeds`. That table has one pool that only rolls when the tool cannot `shears_dig` (`CanItemPerformAbility` inverted, the same ability the vanilla grass table checks) and with a random chance (example 6 percent); its entries are weighted: the four small seeds 3 each, Onion and Horseradish 1 each.
- `village_seeds`: targets the five village house chests (plains, desert, savanna, snowy, taiga) and adds `servus_delicacies:chests/village_seeds`: a pool with a chance (example 50 percent) that gives one stack of one to three of a planting item.
The sub tables are generated by a `LootTableSubProvider` with the `BLOCK` and `CHEST` param sets.

### Tags and data maps
`DelicaciesItemTagsProvider` writes `c:crops/<crop>` and `c:seeds/<crop>` from `CropKind`, adds those sub tags to `c:crops` and `c:seeds` (the NeoForge convention, `Tags.Items.CROPS` and `SEEDS` include per crop sub tags), puts every planting item in `minecraft:villager_plantable_seeds` and the small seed items in `minecraft:chicken_food`. Mustard Seeds are in both `c:crops/mustard` and `c:seeds/mustard`, since they are both the harvest and what is planted. `DelicaciesBlockTagsProvider` puts the six crop blocks into `minecraft:crops` (which also feeds `minecraft:bee_growables`) and `minecraft:maintains_farmland`. `DelicaciesDataMapProvider` writes `neoforge:compostables`: seeds 0.3 like Wheat Seeds, vegetables 0.65 like a Carrot.

### Block states and models
The crop blocks use the vanilla `minecraft:block/crop` parent (four planes at 4 and 12 on X and Z, no two sharing a plane), one model per stage `block/<crop>_stage<n>`, and a variant block state per age through `CropStages`. Verified in 26.1: there is no per block render layer registration for vanilla crops any more (`ItemBlockRenderTypes` is gone; terrain uses an alpha cutout pipeline), so transparent pixels in the stage textures cut out without extra code. Planting and harvest items use `basicItem` models.

### Textures
`tools/delicacies-textures/gen.py` gains the mockup painter (`Spr`, `ellipse`, `capsule`, `poly`, `boundary`, `paint`) without Pillow, and writes `textures/item/` and `textures/block/`. The ported item sprites `kraut_head` (Cabbage and Red Cabbage), `zwiebel` (Onion), `petersilie` (Parsley) and `kren` (Horseradish) are pixel identical to the mockup. New: Mustard Seeds (a heap of yellow brown seeds), three seed items in the colours of their crop, and four stage textures per crop: a shared seedling look, then the crop's own young, grown and ripe plant (cabbage heads with outer leaves, onion tubes over a bulb, parsley sprigs, broad horseradish leaves over a root crown, mustard with yellow flowers then pods).

### Settles from PLAN.md
PLAN.md lists `m14-crops` with the six crops and nothing else; nothing in section 5 is touched.

## Assumptions

- Raw vegetables are not edible in this change. The family has no data driven food values yet and the brief wants hunger and saturation in datapack JSON; `m14-bakery` decides how food values are loaded, and raw vegetables can join then. For the same reason the vegetables are not in `c:foods/vegetable` yet, since the convention means edible foods.
- Onion, Horseradish and Mustard plant themselves; Cabbage, Red Cabbage and Parsley have seed items.
- Mustard's harvest is Mustard Seeds; there is no separate mustard plant item.
- First seeds come from grass and village house chests, not from wandering traders or zombies. Example chances: 6 percent per grass block, 50 percent per house chest.
- Block names: "Cabbages", "Red Cabbages", "Onions", "Parsley", "Horseradish", "Mustard", like vanilla "Carrots" (shown by Jade and similar mods).
- All crops grow at wheat speed with eight ages; none is slowed like beetroot.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Grass drops get crowded in a big pack] -> the modifier is data; a pack removes or tunes it with a datapack.
- [Stage textures drawn without a mockup] -> the play check looks at every stage; the generator is the single source to adjust.
- [A GameTest that rolls loot many times is random] -> it rolls enough times that a miss has a chance far below one in a million, and the chest test rolls the sub table directly.
