# Design

## Context

See proposal.md for the why. `m9-calciner-multiblock` built the pattern this change follows: a pure layout (`CalcinerLayout`), a Minecraft side structure class (`CalcinerStructure`: check, form, unform, `neighbourChanged`), ports by block (`CalcinerPorts`, `RegisterCapabilitiesEvent.registerBlock`), a schematic generated from the layout and anchored on its bottom middle cell (Modonomicon's Visualize anchors one block above the clicked block), and GameTests that build, form and break. Vanilla blocks that stay vanilla while formed (the Calciner's Fluid Tanks) are checked from their formed neighbours in `neighborChanged`.

The Coagulator today is a `MachineBlock`/`MachineBlockEntity` with a menu (recipe popup, gauges, side panel), `PrincipleTanks` taking principles through sides by the Fluids layer, and the production capability.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Reuse the Calciner's approach with as little new machinery as possible.
- Shared pure geometry for every build.

**Non-Goals:**
- A generic multiblock framework; two builds share geometry and the shape of the code, not a base class. A framework can come when the Athanor and later builds show what is really common.

## Decisions

### Shared geometry
`alchemy/structure/Facing` and `alchemy/structure/Offset` (pure) replace `CalcinerLayout.Facing` and `CalcinerLayout.Offset`; `alchemy/structure/Structures` (Minecraft side) holds `facing(Direction)`, `direction(Facing)` and `pos(controller, right, up, facing)`. The Calciner classes and tests switch to them; behaviour does not change.

### Layout
`alchemy/coagulator/CoagulatorLayout` (pure): cells `COAGULATOR (0,0)`, `STONE (-1,0) (1,0)`, `VAT (-1,1) (0,1) (1,1)`; the Vat at right -1 holds Sulfur, 0 Salt, 1 Mercury, matching the Calciner where Sulfur is left and Mercury right. `patternRows()` gives `VVV`, `S0S` with `0` the Coagulator, which is also the bottom middle, so the Visualize anchor stands on the clicked ground without a remap. JUnit `CoagulatorLayoutTest`.

### Blocks
- `CoagulatorBlock` gains `FORMED`; `useItemOn` forms with the Journal (no book), `useWithoutItem` opens the menu only when formed, `onWrench` passes, `onRemove` unforms, `neighborChanged` checks the Stone Bricks.
- `alchemy/coagulator/VatBlock` with `FACING`, `FORMED`, `PIECE` (0 to 2, the Vat cell); no block entity. `neighborChanged` checks the Stone Bricks below the outer Vats. A filled principle bucket on a formed Vat pours with `FluidUtil.interactWithFluidHandler` into the Vat's single tank view.
- Stone Bricks stay vanilla: no shell block. They are checked from their formed neighbours (the Coagulator beside them, the Vat above).

### Ports
Registered with `registerBlock`:
- `FluidHandler.BLOCK` on the Vat: a fill only view of the Coagulator's tank of the Vat's principle (`PrincipleTanks.view` gives a fill only view because the Coagulator's tanks take and never give).
- `ItemHandler.BLOCK` on the Coagulator block: slot 0 the ingredient, insert only; slot 1 the output, extract only.
- `PRODUCTION` stays on the Coagulator block entity, reporting nothing while unformed.
The block entity registrations for the item and fluid handlers go; the Coagulator's `sideTanks()` returns nothing and its item sides lock Off.

### GUI
`CoagulatorScreen`'s layout sets `sidePanel` false. The menu, the popup and the gauges stay; gauges still pour and fill buckets. A GUI less recipe choice is left for the structure discussion.

### Look
- The Coagulator's formed model: a stone casting table with a mold, from vanilla textures.
- Vat models: a copper vat with an open top; formed, a copper spout runs from each Vat down and toward the mold (overhanging into the cell below, never into a plane of another face).
- `VatRenderer`? The Vats have no block entity, so the Coagulator's renderer draws all three Vat levels with offsets from the layout, like the Calciner draws the Receiver. The Coagulator's tanks sync to the client when a drawn level changes by a sixteenth.

### Journal
The Coagulator page gets a multiblock page with Visualize (generated by `ServusManufacturingMultiblockProvider` from `CoagulatorLayout`), the Vat recipe, and text on the Vats and on forming.

### Pure vs Minecraft
Pure: `CoagulatorLayout`, the shared `Facing` and `Offset` (covered by the layout tests). GameTests in `CoagulatorGameTests`: forming in all facings, a missing Vat named, breaking a Stone Bricks block unforms, each Vat takes only its principle, a bucket on the wrong Vat does nothing, items in and out at the Coagulator, unformed does nothing, the schematic accepts the build. Existing Coagulator GameTests build and form the structure first.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The GUI remains, against "mostly no GUI"] → Recipe choice is the one thing a GUI does well here; flagged for the discussion.
- [Vanilla Stone Bricks can be swapped for another block between neighbour updates] → Every removal or placement next to a formed block triggers the check; nothing else can change them.
