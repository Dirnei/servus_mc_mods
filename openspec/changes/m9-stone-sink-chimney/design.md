# Design: m9-stone-sink-chimney

## Context

See proposal.md for why. This change builds on `m0-material-kinds` (stone kind, `#servus_foundation:stones`, `Material`, `MaterialSlot`, `MaterialRecipe`, `MaterialRecipes`, `MaterialBlockItem`, `MaterialModels`) and must be applied after it.

Current state in `foundation/.../machine/sink/`:
- `SinkBlock` is a `BaseEntityBlock`; every one of the eight blocks has a `SinkBlockEntity`, and only part 0 (the controller) owns the buffer. Forming (`tryForm`) changes the block state of the same block, so each block entity survives forming and unforming (`LevelChunk.setBlockState` keeps the block entity when the block stays the same).
- `ChimneyBlock` is a plain `Block` with the state `TOP`; placing or removing one walks down to the Sink and calls `recountChimney`. The Sink draws the smoke in `SinkBlock.animateTick` (`CAMPFIRE_COSY_SMOKE`) above its tallest column while active.
- Models in `datagen/SinkModels`: formed parts and the chimney draw their brick faces with `minecraft:block/bricks`. The unformed casing uses one custom texture `sink_casing` with the bricks painted into the iron frame. The cap top uses `chimney_top`.
- Recipes in `datagen/MachineDataParts`: Sink `BBB/BRB/BBB` (Bricks, iron bars, 8), Chimney `B B/B B/B B` (brick items, 2). Loot in `MachineBlockLoot` is `dropSelf`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Sink and Chimney in any stone of the tag, one stone per formed Sink, mixed stones on a Sink's chimney.
- A free standing chimney with smoke and redstone settings, driven only by events.

**Non-Goals:**
- Any change to the Sink's rates, buffer, GUI or its own smoke.
- A chimney block entity that ticks.

## Decisions

### D1 New capability `foundation/chimney`
The free standing chimney has its own block states, carrot actions, redstone and smoke, none of which concern the Sink. Putting them into `foundation/sink` would make the Sink spec describe a block that works without any Sink. `foundation/sink` keeps everything about a column on a Sink (counting, look, the Sink's smoke) and points to `foundation/chimney` from "Chimney without a Sink". `foundation/materials` gains a requirement listing the stone kind blocks, as it lists the wood blocks.

### D2 Recipes and counts
Both recipes go through `MaterialRecipes.output(output, registries, MaterialKind.STONE)` with the key `#servus_foundation:stones`.
- Sink: `BBB/BRB/BBB`, stone and iron bars, 8 blocks, as today.
- Chimney: `B B/B B/B B`, stone blocks, **6** blocks. Today six brick items (one and a half Bricks blocks) give 2. A stone block is worth four brick items, so 6 blocks for 2 would cost four times as much; 6 for 6 makes one stone block per Chimney, close to the old cost and easy to remember.

The result carries the stone picked by `MaterialPick` (the stone that appears most often, the middle one on a tie). Recipe JSON stays datagen output, so the counts can be retuned by a datapack.

### D3 Stone per block
- `SinkBlockEntity` implements `MaterialHolder` with a `MaterialSlot` on every part, forwarded from `applyImplicitComponents`, `collectImplicitComponents`, `removeComponentsFromTag`, `saveAdditional`, `loadAdditional`, the update tag and `getModelData`. The slot sits next to the buffer; non controller parts only carry the slot.
- `ChimneyBlock` becomes a `BaseEntityBlock` with a new `ChimneyBlockEntity` (`MaterialSlot` only, no ticker) registered in `MachineContent` as `chimney`.
- `MachineContent` registers both items as `MaterialBlockItem` and calls `Material.register(id, MaterialKind.STONE)` for both blocks.
- `MachineBlockLoot` copies the material component from the block entity for both (`CopyComponentsFunction.copyComponentsFromBlockEntity(...).include(MaterialContent.MATERIAL.get())`, as `AdapterBlockLoot` does). The sneak carrot dismantle of a Sink block pops `Material.stamp(new ItemStack(this), blockEntity)`.

### D4 Looks
`MaterialModels` wraps both blocks with the stone skin, whose placeholder is `minecraft:block/bricks`; every quad on that sprite shows the block's stone and the particle sprite is the stone's.
- Formed Sink parts and the Chimney shaft already draw their brick faces with `minecraft:block/bricks`: no model change.
- Unformed casing: the bricks are painted into `sink_casing.png`, so it cannot be retextured. It becomes a stone cube inset by 0.05 px (`0.05` to `15.95`, faces in `minecraft:block/bricks`, cullface on every side) under a full cube of a new cutout overlay `sink_casing_frame.png` (the iron frame of `sink_casing.png` with the brick pixels cleared), generated by `tools/sink-textures/gen.py`. Render type `cutout` for the casing model.
- Cap top: `chimney_top.png` is drawn over the cap's whole 12 px top face. If it paints a brick rim around the flue (checked in task 3.2), the top face is split on the same plane into four rim faces in `minecraft:block/bricks` and one flue face in the inner part of `chimney_top` (renamed `chimney_flue.png`), so the rim takes the stone and the flue stays dark. If it has no brick rim, it stays as it is. Applied: it paints a rim three texture pixels wide (2.25 px on the 12 px cap), so the cap top is split; the flue face spans 4.25 to 11.75 with the UVs 3 to 13 of `chimney_flue.png`. `sink_casing.png` and `chimney_top.png` are gone; `gen.py` holds the frame and the flue as pixel patterns and writes both new textures.

Coplanar face check (CLAUDE.md):
- casing stone cube and frame overlay: the cube is inset 0.05 px, so no faces share a plane;
- cap rim faces and flue face: same plane, but they do not overlap;
- formed parts, shaft and cap sides: unchanged geometry, already checked in earlier changes.

### D5 One stone to form
`SinkBlock.tryForm` keeps its candidate loop. For a candidate whose eight blocks are all unformed Sink blocks it reads each part's stone (`MaterialSlot.material()`, Bricks when none) in part order and asks the pure `SinkStones.distinct(List<String>)` for the distinct ids in first seen order. One id: form, as today. More: remember this list and try the next candidate. After the loop, a remembered list gives the action bar message `message.servus_foundation.sink.mixed_stone` ("All eight Sink blocks need the same stone; found %s") with the block names joined by ", "; otherwise the existing `incomplete` message. Reading eight block entities happens once per carrot click, not per tick.

### D6 Chimney block states
`ChimneyBlock` states: `TOP` (as today), `SMOKE` (`off`, `thin`, `cosy`, `signal`), `REDSTONE` (`always`, `powered`, `unpowered`), `POWERED` (the column has a signal) and `ON_SINK` (the column stands on a formed Sink). `POWERED` and `ON_SINK` hold the same value on every block of a column, so the top block decides its smoke from its own state, without walking the column on the client. 96 states, one model choice (`TOP`) in the blockstate file. The settings live in the block state because the user asked for it and because the client needs them for `animateTick` without a block entity sync.

The two property enums `ChimneySmoke` and `ChimneyRedstone` implement `StringRepresentable` (created with `EnumProperty.create(name, Class)`, verified in 26.1 `EnumProperty`). Each maps to a constant of the pure rules in D9. `ChimneySmoke` also names its particle.

### D7 Events, no scanning
A column walk goes down and up from a block through Chimney blocks, bounded by `SinkBlockEntity.MAX_COLUMN` (32) in each direction. It runs only on these events, on the server:
- `ON_SINK`: `getStateForPlacement` and `updateShape` for `Direction.DOWN` set it to the value of the block below when that is a Chimney, else to `SinkBlock.isFormed(below)`. A changed state makes vanilla update the shape of the block above (`Block.updateOrDestroy` keeps the neighbour shape updates, limit 512), so a Sink forming or unforming under a column passes up the whole column without a walk. Forming and unforming already use `setBlock` with `UPDATE_ALL`.
- `POWERED`: `neighborChanged(BlockState, Level, BlockPos, Block, Orientation, boolean)` (26.1 signature, verified in `BlockBehaviour` and used by `RedstoneLampBlock`) walks the column and checks `Level.hasNeighborSignal` on each block. When the result differs from the block's `POWERED`, every block of the column is set with `Block.UPDATE_CLIENTS`, which sends no neighbour updates and so cannot loop. `onPlace` and `affectNeighborsAfterRemoval` run the same check for the column, and for both halves of a split column.
- `SMOKE` and `REDSTONE`: set on every block of the column by the carrot (D8).

Existing `notifySink` on place and removal stays. `onPlace` also sets `ON_SINK` from the block below when a Chimney was put there without `getStateForPlacement` (commands, GameTests), so such a block joins the column's Sink state too.

### D8 Carrot
`ChimneyBlock` implements `Wrenchable`. On the client `onWrench` returns `SUCCESS`. On the server:
- `ON_SINK`: nothing changes; the action bar shows `message.servus_foundation.chimney.sink` ("The Sink controls this chimney's smoke").
- plain click: the next smoke setting after the clicked block's is set on every block of the column; the action bar shows `message.servus_foundation.chimney.smoke` with the setting name.
- sneak click: the same for the redstone mode, with `message.servus_foundation.chimney.redstone`.

It never dismantles; the block is mined like any block.

### D9 Pure rules
`ChimneyRules` (no Minecraft imports) holds plain enums `Smoke` and `Mode` and:
- `nextSmoke(Smoke)` and `nextMode(Mode)`, wrapping at the end;
- `smokes(Smoke, Mode, boolean powered, boolean onSink)`: false on a Sink or when the setting is off; otherwise true for always, `powered` for while powered and `!powered` for while unpowered.

`SinkStones.distinct(List<String>)` returns the distinct ids in first seen order. Both are covered by JUnit.

### D10 Copy on stack
`getStateForPlacement` copies `SMOKE`, `REDSTONE`, `POWERED` and `ON_SINK` from the block below when it is a Chimney. Otherwise it sets off, always, unpowered and the Sink check. `onPlace` then runs the power check, because the new block may sit next to a signal itself. A block placed between two columns joins them. The upper part keeps its settings until the next carrot click, and the top block's settings decide the smoke.

### D11 Smoke on the client
`ChimneyBlock.animateTick` (client only, `Block.animateTick`) returns unless `TOP` and `ChimneyRules.smokes(...)` hold for its state. Then it adds particles above the cap:
- thin: one or two `ParticleTypes.SMOKE` with `level.addParticle`;
- cosy: `CAMPFIRE_COSY_SMOKE` with `addAlwaysVisibleParticle`, like the Sink;
- signal: `CAMPFIRE_SIGNAL_SMOKE` the same way (all three verified in 26.1 `ParticleTypes`; `CampfireBlock` uses the same two campfire types and call).

There is no ticker and no server work. The Sink's own smoke in `SinkBlock.animateTick` is unchanged.

### Assumptions
- `SinkBlock.tryForm` returns the refusal message (null when it formed), so the carrot shows the reason the cube found: a top or bottom face, no full cube, or mixed stones.
- Recipe book unlocks: the Sink with iron bars, the Chimney with cobblestone, since a tag cannot be an unlock item.
- Chimney recipe output 6 (D2).
- Action bar texts as in D5 and D8; setting names "Off", "Thin smoke", "Cosy smoke", "Signal smoke", "Always", "While powered", "While unpowered".
- Particle counts per `animateTick`: thin 1 to 2, cosy 1 to 2, signal 1 to 2, upward speed as the Sink (0.07).
- Column walk bound 32 in each direction (the Sink's `MAX_COLUMN`); a taller free standing column follows the power of its nearest 32 blocks.
- A Sink and its Chimneys of different stones stack separately in the inventory, because the stone is an item component.

### Pure logic and tests
- JUnit: `ChimneyRulesTest` (step order and wrap for both enums, the smoke rule table including on Sink), `SinkStonesTest` (one stone, two stones in first seen order, defaults counted as given).
- GameTests: `SinkGameTests` gains `mixedStonesRefuseForming`, `oneStoneForms`, `missingStoneCountsAsBricks`, `sinkDropKeepsStone`, `stoneDoesNotChangeRates`. New `ChimneyGameTests`: `carrotStepsSmokeOverColumn`, `sneakCarrotStepsRedstoneMode`, `redstoneOnAnyBlockPowersColumn`, `newChimneyCopiesBelow`, `carrotIgnoredOnSink`, `settingsReturnAfterUnform`, `chimneyOnSinkStillCounts`, `chimneyKeepsStoneOnDrop`. Smoke itself is client only and checked in the play check.
- Manual: the play check.

### PLAN.md
No open decision of PLAN.md is settled here; PLAN.md needs no edit. The stone kind for other blocks is not an open point yet and is not added.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [96 chimney states] → Small; the blockstate file maps them to two models with `forAllStates`.
- [Block state changes with `UPDATE_CLIENTS` skip neighbour updates, so an observer next to a chimney does not see a power or setting change] → Accepted; chimneys are decoration.
- [`hasNeighborSignal` reads strong and weak power from all six sides, so a chimney beside a powered block counts as powered] → That is the vanilla meaning of "receives a redstone signal", as for the Redstone Lamp.
- [Dev worlds hold chimneys without a block entity] → Unreleased; a missing block entity shows Bricks, and replacing the block fixes it.
