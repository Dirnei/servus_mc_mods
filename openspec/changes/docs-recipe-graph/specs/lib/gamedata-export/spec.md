# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Data run writes the game data file
The Manufacturing data run SHALL write `docs/src/data/gamedata.json` at the repository root. The file SHALL contain a schema version number, the mod version, and sections for machines, recipes, crafting recipes, deposits, techs and items. The file SHALL be committed like other datagen output.

#### Scenario: Data run
- **WHEN** `gradlew :manufacturing:runData` finishes
- **THEN** `docs/src/data/gamedata.json` exists and its `schema` field is `2`

### Requirement: Deposits, techs and items are exported
The export SHALL list each deposit with its block and yield, each tech with its parents, cost, `ticks_per_pack` (default applied) and unlocks, and each item referenced anywhere in the file with its display name. Servus item names SHALL come from the English language files of all four Servus mods, including mods that are not on the Manufacturing classpath; vanilla names SHALL come from the vanilla English language data.

#### Scenario: Tech with default lab time
- **WHEN** a tech file omits `ticks_per_pack`
- **THEN** its exported `ticks_per_pack` is the default

#### Scenario: Display names
- **WHEN** the export references `servus_manufacturing:iron_gear` and `minecraft:iron_ingot`
- **THEN** their names are "Iron Gear" and "Iron Ingot"

#### Scenario: Storage item name
- **WHEN** the export references `servus_storage:shuttle`
- **THEN** its name is the one in the Storage English language file, not a title cased fallback

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Crafting recipes of all Servus mods are exported
The export SHALL list, in a crafting section separate from the production recipes, every recipe generated by Lib, Logistics, Manufacturing and Storage that is not an assembling, crushing, smelting or blasting recipe. Each entry SHALL have its id, type, inputs summed per item or tag with counts, and outputs with counts. Production recipes and the vanilla cooking selection SHALL NOT change because of crafting entries.

#### Scenario: Power cable
- **WHEN** the Logistics power cable recipe uses six copper ingots, two redstone and one glass for eight cables
- **THEN** its crafting entry has inputs copper ingot 6, redstone 2, glass 1 and output power cable 8

#### Scenario: Chain data unchanged
- **WHEN** the Storage shuttle recipe is exported
- **THEN** the production recipes section does not contain it and no vanilla recipe is added because of its inputs

### Requirement: Shaped recipes keep their grid
A crafting entry built on a grid SHALL also carry its pattern rows and the item or tag for each pattern symbol. This SHALL hold for vanilla shaped recipes and for Servus recipe types that use a shaped grid. Shapeless entries SHALL carry no pattern. Tag ingredients SHALL be exported as tags.

#### Scenario: Heavy rack
- **WHEN** the Storage heavy rack recipe uses its own recipe type with pattern `I I`, `PPP`, `I I`
- **THEN** its entry has those three rows, `I` as iron ingot, `P` as the planks tag, and inputs iron ingot 4 and planks tag 3

#### Scenario: Shapeless zone label
- **WHEN** the zone label recipe is shapeless with paper and the dyes tag
- **THEN** its entry has inputs paper 1 and dyes tag 1 and no pattern

### Requirement: Special recipes are marked
A recipe without fixed ingredients or result, such as the facade recipe, SHALL be exported as a crafting entry marked special, with its id and type and empty inputs and outputs.

#### Scenario: Facade
- **WHEN** the Logistics facade recipe is exported
- **THEN** its entry is marked special and has no inputs and no outputs
