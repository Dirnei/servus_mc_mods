## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Gantry placed over a rail
**Reason**: The Fluid Gantry is replaced by the Fluid Rack, the Fluid Inbound and the Fluid Outbound, which work like the item side.
**Migration**: Place a Fluid Rack, a Fluid Inbound or a Fluid Outbound beside a rail. Placed gantries disappear with their fluid; empty them before updating.

### Requirement: Gantry joins by its rail
**Reason**: The Fluid Gantry is replaced by the Fluid Rack, the Fluid Inbound and the Fluid Outbound, which work like the item side.
**Migration**: Fluid Racks join through the rail in front of their port bay, fluid stations through the rail in front of their lower half. Placed gantries disappear with their fluid; empty them before updating.

### Requirement: Two built-in tanks
**Reason**: The Fluid Gantry is replaced by the Fluid Rack, the Fluid Inbound and the Fluid Outbound, which work like the item side.
