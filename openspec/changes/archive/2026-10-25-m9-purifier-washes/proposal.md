# Proposal

## Why

The Purifier washes exactly one thing today: Ash into Salt. It sits idle once the Ash Pan is empty, Salt has only one source, and the Coagulator has nothing that a later change can use as a first seed of Salt, Sulfur or Mercury. Water washing is old craft: sugar boiled down leaves rock sugar, saltpetre is leached out of gunpowder earth, cinnabar is washed out of red ore, and dyers wash colour out of cloth. The Purifier should do all of that.

## What Changes

- Three new items made only by the Purifier, without a crafting recipe: Kandis (rock sugar crystal), Brimstone and Cinnabar. They are the first seeds the Coagulator change after this one uses.
- A purification recipe may make 0 mB Salt and only a residue: a wash. The residue slot, the Residue Crock, the Jade tooltip and the drawn heap handle any residue item, not only Caput Mortuum.
- New shipped purification recipes (numbers are datapack examples):
  - Seeds: Sugar gives a little Salt and Kandis; Gunpowder gives Salt (the saltpetre) and Brimstone; Redstone Dust gives a little Salt and Cinnabar.
  - Salt: Dried Kelp gives Salt (the good early source); Rotten Flesh gives Salt and Leather; Soul Sand gives a little Salt and Sand; Magma Cream gives a Slimeball and a little Salt.
  - Washes without Salt: each of the 16 Concrete Powders gives its Concrete; each dyed Wool, Carpet and Terracotta gives the undyed one. One recipe per colour, generated by a datagen loop.
- The Ash Pan keeps reading its Salt per Ash from the first purification recipe that takes Ash; none of the new recipes takes Ash.
- The Purifier book entry lists the new recipes from the same table that generates them, the colour washes as one line per kind.
- JEI's Purification category shows the residue and leaves the Salt out for a wash.
- The Vat's floor and the Crock's heap show the texture of the block being washed or left behind, so a Crock of White Wool does not look like Caput Mortuum.
- GameTests for a seed wash (Sugar to Kandis) and a wash without Salt (Red Concrete Powder to Red Concrete).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `alchemy/purifier`: recipes may make no Salt, the Vat and the Crock take and show any item, washes report no Salt; new requirements for the seed items, the shipped seed, Salt and colour recipes and the book list.
- `alchemy/recipe-viewer`: a requirement for the Purification category, which exists but was never specified, now with the residue.

## Impact

- `ServusAlchemyItems`: `KANDIS`, `BRIMSTONE`, `CINNABAR`; item models, lang and textures (generator in `tools/purifier-textures/gen.py`).
- `AlchemyRecipeTable.purification()` grows; a new `washes()` loop over `DyeColor`; `ServusAlchemyRecipeProvider` writes all of them, the residue optional.
- `PurifierBlockEntity`, `PurifierRenderer`, the Jade Purifier provider: any input and residue item.
- `PurificationRecipeCategory`: residue slot, no Salt slot for a wash.
- `WorkshopCategory.purifier()`: the list from the table, a pure line formatter `PurificationText` with JUnit.
- GameTests in `PurifierGameTests`.
- `alchemy/CHANGELOG.md` gets its line at archive time.

## Non-goals

- Ore washing or anything the Coagulator already makes from the principles (the Slimeball from Magma Cream is the one exception the plan asks for).
- A same colour mechanic or a tag based wash; each colour is its own recipe.
- Using Kandis, Brimstone or Cinnabar anywhere: that is `m9-coagulator-seeds`.
