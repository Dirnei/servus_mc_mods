# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Calciner parts
Alchemy SHALL add the Firebox, Ash Pan, Alembic, Receiver, Sulfur Hood and Copper Duct as blocks, each crafted at a crafting table from vanilla materials without research. The Calciner block itself SHALL be the furnace mouth.

#### Scenario: Alembic recipe
- **WHEN** the player crafts the Alembic pattern of copper ingots and glass
- **THEN** one Alembic is produced
