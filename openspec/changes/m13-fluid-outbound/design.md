# Design: m13-fluid-outbound

## Context

After the three earlier changes of this stack, Shuttles has:
- Fluid Racks as fluid stores;
- a Fluid Inbound on a shared two-high `FluidStationBlock`/`FluidStationBlockEntity` base with a fluids-only side layer;
- `FluidStoreRef` and a generalised put-away loop;
- `FluidActivity` lamp reasons;
- the tanker coupling at ports.

The Fluid Gantry still serves as Import, Storage and Export dock.

Restock today lives in `WarehouseNetwork.restockFluids` and `restockSources`, planned by `FluidRestockPlan`. It reads Export gantry tanks that are locked and have a keep level (`FluidGantryBlockEntity.keep`, `restockKeep`). Its keep buttons are in `FluidGantryMenu` and `FluidGantryScreen`. The item Outbound Station keeps ghost items with amounts in `keep` `ItemSlots`, set through `KeepSlotPayload`.

The gantry footprint is listed in the agent survey recorded in the proposal's Impact section. That covers the package, the registries, the payload, the renderer, the screen, the menu, the datagen, the lang, `gantry.json`, the textures, `tools/gantry-textures/`, the GameTests and the fixtures.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- The Fluid Outbound on the shared station base, with a keep and restock from Fluid Racks.
- The Fluid Gantry fully gone from code, data, assets, tests, docs and specs.
- The tanker test suite rebuilt on the new blocks with the same coverage.

**Non-Goals:**
- A converter for placed gantries.
- New fluid features beyond the gantry's (feed command, Storage Terminal fluids).

## Decisions

### Outbound on the station base

`FluidOutboundBlockEntity extends FluidStationBlockEntity`:
- `fluidSides` is `OUTBOUND_MODES`, with fallback Off and BACK set to PUSH in the constructor, as `OutboundStationBlockEntity` does;
- its one tank has a `FluidKeep(fluid, amount)`;
- the tank's `FluidSlot` validator is "the keep fluid is set and equal";
- `sideTankFluid(i)` returns the content, otherwise the keep fluid, so the panel chip shows what the tank is for.

Push and Output sides drain through `FluidSideHandler`, which refuses fills because the modes allow none. `FluidSideMover` pushes on Push sides.

The keep is saved as `"KeepFluid"` and `"KeepAmount"`, kept on the dropped item, and read by the side panel label.

### Keep cell in the GUI

- `FluidOutboundMenu` has one ghost keep cell above the gauge.
  - Clicking a cell with a cursor stack that is a filled container reads its fluid through `Capabilities.Fluid.ITEM` on a copy and sends `SetFluidKeepPayload(tank, fluid, amount)`. An empty cursor clears the cell.
  - The step buttons use the Foundation number stepper widget that the gantry keep steps use today, at -1 B, +1 B, -10 B and +10 B.
- The server clamps the amount to `[0, capacity]`.
- The gauge draws a keep mark as the gantry gauge does.

### Look and recipe

The Fluid Outbound shares the tank, the wood and the lamp copying of the Fluid Inbound (`m13-fluid-inbound` design: Look, Wood, Recipe), with the Outbound Station's block models as its upper half. Its recipe is an Outbound Station on top of a Fluid Tank, a wood material recipe that keeps the station's wood.

### Restock targets

`restockFluids` iterates the Outbound tanks with a keep fluid and an amount above 0 instead of Export gantry tanks. `FluidRestockPlan` stays as it is. Sources are rack sections only (`restockSources` filters to `FluidStoreRef` kind RACK).

The lamp uses `FluidActivity` with the `missing` and `noTanker` reasons. The "no tanker reaches" check is `fleet.tankerReaches(station)`, which Import tanks already use. That closes the PLAN.md open point for exports.

A restock run whose Outbound is removed or loses its front rail brings its cargo back by `fluidTargets`. This is the existing leftover path, with the station dropped from the candidates.

### Removing the gantry

**Delete:**
- the `gantry` package, except the pure planners;
- `FluidGantryMenu`, `FluidGantryScreen` and `FluidGantryRenderer`;
- `GantryModels`, `SetGantryLockPayload` and its registration;
- `GANTRY_CONTENTS`;
- the `FLUID_GANTRY` registry entries and the creative tab entry;
- `NodeKind.GANTRY` and its rule branches;
- `gantries`, `pendingGantries`, `storageGantries`, `tankViews` gantry code, and the Host methods `gantryMode` and `gantryPumping`;
- `Shuttle.pumpingIntoGantry` and `FleetSaves` gantry fields;
- `gantry.json` with its `ShuttlesStatsManager` fields;
- the gantry lang keys and textures;
- the generated gantry assets, regenerated by `runData`;
- `tools/gantry-textures/`;
- the four gantry GameTest classes and `GantryFixtures`.

`GantryTanks` goes with the gantry. `FluidRouting` no longer needs it after `m13-fluid-rack`.

**Move:** `FluidRouting`, `FluidPutAwayPlan`, `FluidRestockPlan` and `FluidStoreRef` go to `shuttles/.../fluid/`, together with their JUnit tests.

**Replace:**
- `ElevatorGameTests.railRun` uses `GantryFixtures.shape`. That helper moves to `RailFixtures.shape`.
- `FluidColumn` (from `m13-fluid-rack`) keeps the column drawing.
- `FluidLockEntries` is unused after the gantry GUI goes and is deleted.

**Rebuild:** `TankerFixtures` builds the scene from a Fluid Inbound, a Fluid Rack and a Fluid Outbound. Every case of `TankerRunGameTests`, `TankerLampGameTests` and `TankerRestockGameTests` is ported, scenario for scenario, to the new blocks. The gantry-only cases are dropped: lock change reroute and Import is no restock source, which becomes Inbound is no restock source.

### Saved worlds

Released Shuttles versions (0.2.0 and 0.3.0) ship the gantry. After this change a placed gantry is an unknown block and becomes air on load, and its fluid is lost. The `shuttles/CHANGELOG.md` Unreleased entry says so under Changed, and tells players to empty their gantries before updating. No data fixer is written (proposal Non-goals).

### Specs and PLAN.md

- The `shuttles/tanker` Purpose line in the main spec is updated by hand at archive time, because a delta cannot change it. It becomes: "Tanker shuttles carry fluids over the warehouse rails: from Fluid Inbounds to Fluid Racks with room, and from Fluid Racks to Fluid Outbounds that keep a fluid."
- `shuttles/fluid-gantry` is left with no requirements after archive, and its spec folder is deleted in the archive commit.
- PLAN.md M13 loses the gantry sentence and the Export red lamp point.

## PLAN.md decisions settled

- The M13 outbound dock is a two-high Fluid Outbound with per-tank keeps.
- The gantry is removed without conversion.
- The open point about the red lamp for unreachable exports is closed by the Outbound's lamp rule.

## NeoForge APIs

The same APIs as `m13-fluid-inbound`. In addition:
- `Capabilities.Fluid.ITEM` reads the keep fluid from the cursor stack;
- `CustomPacketPayload` with `StreamCodec` for `SetFluidKeepPayload`, registered in `ServusShuttlesPayloads` like `KeepSlotPayload`.

## Pure and Minecraft parts

- **Pure (JUnit):**
  - `FluidRestockPlanTest` and `FluidRoutingTest`, moved, with a rack-only sources case;
  - a `FluidKeepTest` for amount clamping and the "takes only its keep fluid" predicate.
- **GameTests (`FluidOutboundGameTests`):**
  - placement and the break that keeps the keep;
  - the keep fluid guards the tank;
  - a cleared keep keeps its content;
  - Push into a Fluid Tank;
  - Output to a duct on pull;
  - no fill through any side;
  - not counted as stock;
  - the restock lamp: missing, no tanker, green when met;
  - buckets and the potato.
- **Ported tanker suites:** they cover put-away, restock, reroute, recall and lift.
- **Manual play check:** the whole chain pump, Inbound, tanker, Rack, tanker, Outbound, machine; the Outbound Station on the tank; the tanker stop at the Outbound; flicker.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Players lose gantry fluid on update] Mitigation: the changelog warning. The change is played before release, so the warning ships with the version that removes the block.
- [Large deletion touches many tests] The tanker suites are ported case by case before the gantry classes are deleted, so coverage never drops in between.
