# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Athanor schematics
The Athanor entry SHALL show the Athanor on its hearth with its three Fluid Tanks as a multiblock page with Visualize and SHALL tell the player to form it with the Journal. Every shipped tier SHALL have its own multiblock page, on the entry of its stage (Albedo on the Salt Crystal Block entry), showing the hearth, the Athanor, its tanks and the blocks of that tier and the tiers below. The schematics SHALL stand on the clicked ground.

#### Scenario: Albedo page
- **WHEN** a player opens the Salt Crystal Block entry and presses Visualize
- **THEN** a ghost of the diamond hearth with the twelve Salt Crystal Blocks around it, the Athanor and its tanks stands on the clicked ground
