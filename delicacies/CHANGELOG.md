### Added
- The Aquarium: lay Aquarium Floor blocks under a Fluid Tank of water and Aquarium Lids on top, form it with the Configuration Carrot and breed cod or salmon. Feed them seeds, keep the water clean with a Carbon Filter and fresh water, power the lid light and pumps with FE, and take the catch by hand or by pipe. Water drained through the aquarium comes out as Murky Water.
