## How the numbers work

- A machine makes its output once per cycle. Most machines use their `work_ticks`; the Assembler and the Crusher use the recipe time, and the Research Lab uses the tech's `ticks_per_pack`. There are 20 ticks per second.
- Machine counts are rounded up. Utilization says how busy the machines of a node are.
- Burner machines burn fuel only while they work. Powered machines draw power only while they work, so a half busy Assembler draws half its power on average.
- Coal Generators run as much as the power draw needs. Their coal and the burners' coal are added to the coal node, which in turn needs its own extractors and fuel.
- Where an item can be made in more than one way, click its node and pick a route, for example iron ingots from grit.

## Trying a change

1. Open **Production chains** and pick a product and a rate.
2. Change numbers in **Edit numbers**. Changed fields are highlighted; nodes and the totals show the old value next to the new one.
3. Add new items, recipes or concept machines in **Draft content**. Drafted content has a dashed outline. Concept machines are marked **needs code**: they are ideas the mod cannot run until someone builds them.
4. Give the proposal a title and notes.

Your draft is saved in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Sharing and handing over a proposal

- **Share link** puts the whole draft into the link itself. Anyone who opens it sees the same draft. Very large drafts do not fit into a link; use Export for those.
- **Export** downloads `servus-proposal-<title>.json`, and **Copy JSON** copies the same content. The file lists every changed number with its old and new value, the new items, recipes and machines, your notes, and the before and after totals of the chain you were looking at. Hand this file over to have the proposal implemented in the mod.
- **Import** loads a proposal file. If the game data changed since the proposal was written, the affected numbers are listed as conflicts with the proposal's old value, the current value and the proposed value. They are not applied until you choose **Apply anyway**.
