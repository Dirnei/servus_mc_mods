Foundation is the base of the Servus family. Shuttles and Alchemy both need it. It brings the transport blocks every base needs, a handful of utility machines, and the shared tools and rules that make all Servus blocks work the same way. It also works on its own as a small pipes and utilities mod.

## Tools

- **Configuration Carrot**: the wrench of the family. Use it on a face to change that side. Sneak use it to pick up a block and keep its settings. On a duct it opens the duct's screen.
- **Engineer Goggles**: worn on your head. They show side settings and ducts hidden behind facades.
- **Data Potato**: shows how busy the data line is. Use it on a duct, a tank or a Servus storage block for a short report in chat.

## The Utility Duct

One block that carries up to four lines side by side:

| Item | Line | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| Item Pipe | orange | items |
| Power Cable | red | FE |
| Fluid Pipe | blue | fluids |
| Data Cable | white, gray or black by tier | links machines for autocrafting |

Use a line item to place a duct, or use it on a duct to add that line. Each line forms its own network. Ducts work with any block of any mod that offers the standard NeoForge item, energy or fluid capability.

Use the Configuration Carrot on a duct to open its screen. It has a tab for every side that touches a block, and on each tab every line of the duct has its own settings:

- **In**, **Out** and **Pull**, a redstone setting (ignore, while powered, while unpowered) and an On/Off switch.
- A **channel**. Things only move between sides on the same channel. Channels have names, colours and icons, and your team shares them.
- **Filters** and a **priority** on the Item Pipe and the Fluid Pipe. Right click a set item filter slot to give it an amount: keep 64 saplings in a chest, or fill a furnace up to 16 coal.

The item picker of a filter slot searches by name. Type `#` to list item tags with their item count: Tab completes the top hit, and hovering a tag shows some of its items. The fluid picker lists every fluid, also those without a bucket.

A trash can on the tab removes that line from the side.

Ducts can stand in water. **Facades** hide a duct behind the look of any full block: craft a block with a nugget to get six. A facade closes its face. A **Hollow Facade** (eight facades in a ring) covers the face but lets the duct through.

### The data line

The data line moves nothing by itself. It links blocks that make or hold items, so a Shuttles warehouse and its Crafting CPU can find them without setup. It comes in three tiers:

| Cable | Items per second | Directions |
|---|---|---|
| Data Cable | 32 | shared by both directions |
| Duplex Data Cable | 32 | 32 each way |
| Fast Data Cable | 128 | 128 each way |

Use a higher tier cable on a duct to upgrade it in place. Right click a data duct with an empty hand to see its network and its load. Other mods can join the data line; see the [developer guide](/servus_mc_mods/developers/).

## Fluid Tank

A glass tank with a copper frame. Tanks that touch and fill a whole box become one big tank. Buckets work on every face. The block you craft into its bottom becomes the tank's floor.

## Machines

All machines face you when placed, open a screen on right click and keep their settings when you pick them up with the carrot.

- **Coal Generator**: burns furnace fuel into 40 FE per tick. It stops when its buffer is full and starts again below half.
- **Burner Pump** and **Electric Pump**: stand on an infinite water source and fill a buffer without using the water up. The Burner Pump burns fuel, the Electric Pump runs on FE.
- **Incinerator**: eight Incinerator blocks in a 2 by 2 by 2 cube, formed with the carrot. It voids items, fluids and FE. Chimney blocks on a top corner make it faster, and Capacity Upgrades give it a bigger buffer. Incinerator and Chimney take the look of the stone you craft them from.
- **Chimney**: also works alone as decoration. The carrot picks how much it smokes.
- **Ender Anchor**: keeps chunks loaded while it has FE. Anchor Upgrades make the area bigger, and each team has a cap on loaded chunks.
- **Mill**: grinds stone into cobblestone, cobblestone into gravel, gravel into sand, and blocks such as glowstone, quartz, amethyst, clay and sandstone back into their pieces. It also mills sugar cane into sugar, bones into bone meal, blaze rods into powder, wool into string and flowers into dye. It has no power of its own: hold right click on a **Hand Crank**, or put an **Electric Motor** against it. Datapacks can add more milling recipes.
- **Mixer**: an open tub that stirs up to four items, and maybe one fluid, into one result. It makes Mud, Coarse Dirt and Concrete Powder in all 16 colours. Like the Mill it needs a Hand Crank or an Electric Motor on its back or sides. Pour the fluid in with a bucket or pipe it into the back or sides; the result leaves through the chute in front. JEI shows its recipes.
- **Machine Adapter**: puts a furnace, smoker or blast furnace on the data line, so a Crafting CPU can smelt with it. See [Machine Adapter](/servus_mc_mods/developers/machine-adapter/).

Machines a Shuttles warehouse works with over the data line get a number from it, such as "Furnace (Adapter #1)". The pencil in the title row of their screen gives them a name as well.

On a data line with a Shuttles warehouse, the Coal Generator and the Burner Pump keep their fuel slot stocked. They ask for the fuel already in the slot, so a slot of Coal Blocks gets Coal Blocks, and for coal when it is empty. They top up once a batch is missing (8 by default), not after every coal.

## Carbon Filter

Mill Charcoal into **Charcoal Powder**, smelt it into **Activated Charcoal** in a Blast Furnace, and craft two of it with wheat (or flour) and Paper into a **Carbon Filter**. Machines of other mods can wear it down; its bar shows what is left. A worn out filter becomes a **Spent Filter**, which composts.

## Upgrades

- **Speed Upgrade**: doubles the rate of a block with upgrade slots, such as the Packing Station or the Electric Motor.
- **Capacity Upgrade**: gives a block more room, such as the Incinerator.
- **Anchor Upgrade**: makes the Ender Anchor's area bigger and lets a Shuttles warehouse site wake up.

## Side settings

Every Servus machine has the same side panel in its screen. Each side can be Auto, Input, Output, Both, Pull, Push or Off, relative to the block's front, so the settings survive turning it. Blocks with tanks also have a **Fluids** layer. A fluid side takes fluid in (Input, Pull or Off) or gives it out (Push, Output or Off), as the block allows, and reaches all tanks or one picked tank: right click a side cell to pick the tank. A block with one tank offers no tank choice. Right click the air with the carrot to switch between the Items and Fluids layer.

## Config

`config/servus_foundation-common.toml` holds, among others, the team chunk cap of the Ender Anchor (`maxLoadedChunksPerTeam`), whether anchors give way to FTB Chunks (`enderAnchorRespectsFtbChunks`) and whether the carrot may turn blocks of other mods (`wrenchRotatesBlocks`).
